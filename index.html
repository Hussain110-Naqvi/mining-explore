The problem

The experienced eye is the scarcest thing underground.

Every development round needs someone experienced to look at it: after the blast, before re-entry, and after ground support. Those people are leaving faster than they are replaced, so checks get rushed, handed to green workers, or skipped.

~246,400 hires needed across Canadian mining, 2026 to 2035. Source: MiHR 2026 Outlook, baseline scenario.
How a round gets checked today Where it falls short
A supervisor judges it by eye Fewer of them every year, and nothing is recorded for the next crew.
A survey crew scans the heading Needs a trained person at the face for every round, and the numbers reach the crew later.
Scanning built into a new drill rig Tied to one manufacturer's newer rigs. Jacklegs, older machines and mixed fleets are left out.
The crew scans with a tablet One more step in a cycle already short on minutes, so it is the first thing dropped.
Surface fragmentation cameras Designed around open-pit light and GPS. Underground means dust, darkness and no positioning.
Manual photo or sieve checks Slow enough that they are done rarely, so blast design gets little feedback.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

Do we need a new drill rig?+

No. The kit clips onto the machine that mucks the round, and the software also accepts phone scans and survey files. It is meant for jacklegs, older machines and mixed fleets as much as new equipment.

Does the crew have to do anything?+

With the clip-on kit, no. It starts recording when it sees the face and stops when the machine leaves. The phone path is a short scan after mucking, for sites that want to start without mounting anything.

Does it need a network underground?+

No. The kit runs with its radios off and stores everything on board. Data comes out on a removable drive.

Does it replace our survey?+

No. Survey stays the reference. Round Check gives the crew and the supervisor a grade on every round, including the ones survey never gets to.

How accurate is it?+

That is what the underground tests are for. Our grades will be logged before an independent survey of the same round is released, then compared blind. Our target for pull is within 3 cm of survey. We will share the results either way.

Can we try it?+

We are pre-pilot and lining up first sites now. If you run development headings, the most useful thing today is a conversation about how you check a round and what a bad one costs you.
